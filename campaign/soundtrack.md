# SOUNDTRACK — Spotify picks for the Lantern-Keeper
### Play these from Spotify at the table. The app never touches them (licensing). Original music lives in the app; songs from our world live here.

**Build three playlists:** *Beds* (instrumental, shuffle, under scenes) · *Cues* (one song = one moment, never shuffled, tapped by hand) · *Roads* (one per gate, made the week they choose).
**Settings:** Crossfade 12s · Autoplay OFF · Download every playlist.
