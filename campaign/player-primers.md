# YOUR CHARACTERS, AS THE LANTERN-KEEPER SEES THEM
### Five primers — no spoilers, all invitation

*From your DM: this is how each of you lives in my head when I prepare. Read
yours. If anything feels wrong — a detail, a fear, a dream, the way you
talk — TELL ME NOW, while we're early. Nothing here is locked. These
characters are yours; I'm just the weather around them.*

---

## 🐊 PEACHES CAPICHE — Aasimar Druid
**The mermaid on borrowed feet.**

The way I see you: you walk like someone who remembers a different way of
moving. You are warm, wandering, a little untouchable — "wherever the road
bends next" — and you protect things smaller than you without ever deciding
to. The sea did not let go of you; you did not let go of it either. And the
alligator — who followed you out of the water and has never once left —
watches everything, as if he's waiting for something particular. Your song
matters more than you know yet. When you sing, places listen.

What I'll lean into: your search for your tail · your bond with the
alligator · what "home" means to someone from two worlds · moments where
protecting someone costs you something.

Questions for you (answer any, change anything):
1. What do you miss MOST about the sea — a place, a sound, a someone?
2. What's the alligator's name, and what does he do when he's worried?
3. If you got your tail back tomorrow, would you go home — or has the road
   changed what home means?

---

## 🎩 WILLIAM "BLUE-CHEW BILLY" BLACKWOOD — Human Wizard
**The showman whose best trick is the mask.**

The way I see you: sapphire coat, quicker joke, a lozenge for every
occasion — and underneath the performance, a man carrying his late mentor's
handwritten spellbook like a heart outside his body. You joke about
everything so nothing true can land on you. But the book is true. The
people at this table are becoming true. And I notice you check that the
book is still chained to your belt more often than you check your cards.
After what happened at the Dry Anchor, Saltmere has seen a crack in the
mask — and I don't think they liked you less for it.

What I'll lean into: the mentor — his voice, his jokes, his missing pages ·
the tension between the performance and the person · what you do when
sincerity is the only trick that works.

Questions for you:
1. What was your mentor's name, and what's ONE lesson of his you quote
   (or misquote) constantly?
2. What does the show-Billy do that the real Billy would never?
3. Who, before this party, last saw you with the mask fully off?

## 🛡 PHILIP — Halfling Fighter
**The gentle blade.**

The way I see you: always watching, always half a step from the door,
white-haired with a scar you don't explain. Trained from childhood to end
things — raised, somehow, gentle anyway. You hold a line inside yourself
every single day, and what you fear isn't dying: it's drifting from your
own convictions, waking one day as the weapon instead of the man. You said
you came here "following someone." You protect people before you've decided
to; your body votes before your mind does.

What I'll lean into: the discipline and the gentleness pulling against each
other · who taught you, and what they'd think now · standing between danger
and someone who never asked you to.

Questions for you:
1. WHO were you following — and do they know?
2. What's the rule you will not break, even if breaking it would win?
3. What does your love — the one you fear losing — smell like, sound like,
   cook like? Give me one warm detail I can use.

## ⚔ FREYA — Fairy Fighter
**Two swords, black wings, and a room that laughs.**

The way I see you: tattooed, bladed, deliberately a little frightening —
armor worn on the OUTSIDE so nobody checks for the wound underneath. You've
endured something you don't discuss, and it forged one vow: the people you
love will never suffer what you suffered. Not while you breathe. And yet —
your favorite sound is the whole table laughing. Dangerous and beloved are
allowed to be the same thing; you might be the proof. (Also: there are two
Freyas in this party and I think the universe did that on purpose.)

What I'll lean into: menace as a love language · the vow and what it costs
to keep · moments where the room laughs BECAUSE of you, not at you.

Questions for you:
1. Name two people on the list of those you protect — one obvious, one
   surprising.
2. What actually makes YOU laugh?
3. The thing you endured: I will never put it on screen without asking —
   but tell me one EDGE of it you're comfortable with me touching.

## 🌸 FREYA MOON — Fairy Druid
**Springtime with a pendant full of grief.**

The way I see you: rose-gold hair full of flowers, wings like sunrise
stained glass, kindness as a first reflex — and around your neck, in a
crystal pendant, the last living blossom of the Heartbloom Grove, your home
that went silent. You smile easily and mean it, but underneath is someone
carrying an entire lost world, and your true fear isn't death: it's losing
HOPE — the belief that the world can still be beautiful, that the grove can
bloom again. As long as the blossom lives, so does the reason you're kind.

What I'll lean into: wonder as courage · the pendant and what makes it
stir · your quest to restore the grove · the moment the spectacular spell
is also the RIGHT spell.

Questions for you:
1. Describe one perfect memory of the Heartbloom Grove before the blight —
   small and specific.
2. Who did you lose when the grove went quiet — name one.
3. What refills your hope when it runs low?
